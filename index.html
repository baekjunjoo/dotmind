04 · Standard program

The recovery ladder — 8 sessions, 4 stages

50 min / session · 1–2 sessions per week
Facilitator-guided or self-paced at home
Stage 1 · SensationLearning to read by touchPin familiarisation, two-handed scanning, texture & line discrimination
Stage 2 · PerceptionShapes under the fingertipsShape matching, odd-one-out, orientation & size comparison
Stage 3 · CognitionMental maps & memorySpatial layouts, tactile working memory, rotation & symmetry
Stage 4 · FunctionBack to daily lifeRoute planning, daily objects, calendars & clocks — independence restored
